# Releases

Releases are managed by the maintainer. The former release-preparation scripts,
publication wrapper, and Dagger release-artifact command have been removed.

The [0.1.0 scope](docs/release-0.1.0.md) and
[known limitations](docs/known-limitations.md) describe the intended release.
The [preparation record](docs/release-preparation.md) records historical checks,
not a current publication procedure.
