# Expand ownership inventories with preserved dispositions

The initial ownership policy deliberately retained coarse codec facets and a
profile-inventory closure gap. Expanding PNG/APNG exposes concrete sample,
color-signaling and orientation combinations, so an increased gap count is real
newly enumerated work rather than evidence to hide behind the old facet totals.
Keep the umbrella obligations and add source-bound inventory axes and cells;
qualify narrowly scoped vector cells before claiming whole-profile coverage.

Catalog migration is append-only. Authenticate a captured prior catalog against
the existing ledger, require every prior obligation to remain byte-equivalent in
meaning and authority, preserve every prior disposition, and add only explicit
issue-linked gaps. Reject migrations of an approved global ledger. This replaces
neither owner review nor approval, and avoids silently reseeding the ledger or
refreshing its catalog digest after existing criteria have changed. Source-bound
exclusion approvals survive only while their complete obligation and proof
subjects remain valid.
