# Render tier

Scope: `.config/nextest.toml` profile `ci-render` selects
`binary(/^(parity|limits)_/)`: `parity_registered_case_svg.rs` (878 lines, 8
tests), `parity_registered_case_png.rs` (306, 10), `parity_frame_export.rs`
(214, 1), `limits_scene_admission.rs` (110, 2), `limits_scene_scaling.rs`
(160, 3), sharing `common/registered_cases.rs` (743 lines). CI runs them in
debug as slices of the `default` profile across four shards.

## 1. Registry and evidence

`tests/conformance/cases/registry.json` has 37 entries: 35 `kind: scene`, one
typography, one frame. **Every one is `layer: "focused"`.** There are no
real-world, adversarial or regression cases in this registry although
CONTEXT.md defines the corpus as all four. The three real-world scenes under
`tests/conformance/cases/representative/` are judged by `xtask representative`
and are not in the render tier.

`registered_scene_variants` (`registered_cases.rs:62-64`) yields 35 cases →
48 variants (27 cases with one variant; freedraw 3, cjk-propagation 4,
transform-opacity-groups 4, five others with 2). The SVG harness judges three
dimensions per variant (144 judgments), the PNG harness one (48). The
typography case is judged in the fast tier.

Per variant the committed evidence is `official.svg`, `official.png`,
`official-svg.rgba` (Chromium `<img>` raster of the official SVG),
`official-png.rgba` (canvas raster), plus per case `provenance.json`,
`manifest.json`, `manifest.sha256`, `expected-report-{svg,png}.json`,
`scene.excalidraw`. Generated by `tools/reference-oracle/run-conformance.sh`
inside `mcr.microsoft.com/playwright@sha256:111dde95…` with `--network none
--platform linux/amd64`, Chromium 149.0.7827.55, captured twice in fresh
browser processes with identical digests.

Size: `tests/conformance/cases` is 81.1 MB in 489 files; 64.2 MB is `.rgba`.
**The render tier never reads `official-svg.rgba`** (32 MB): both harnesses
read only `png["raw_raster"]["path"]`. `official.png` (1.7 MB) is redundant
with `official-png.rgba`.

## 2. What the SVG harness judges

`svg_divergences` (`parity_registered_case_svg.rs:305-359`) renders each
variant once and judges:

1. **Extent** (`:363-373`): string equality of root `viewBox`, `width`,
   `height`.
2. **Paths** (`:376-397`): every `<path d>` in document order as exact
   strings. Text, fills, colors, opacity and transforms are not in this
   dimension.
3. **Raster** (`:403-498`): both the official SVG and ours rasterized by the
   same in-process workspace `resvg` 0.49.0 / `usvg` 0.49.0 / `tiny-skia`
   0.12.0 / `fontdb` 0.24.0, with a fontdb built from each document's own
   `@font-face` payloads (`:645-681`), compared under `PureRustSvg` with the
   geometry/image budget.

`assert_report_matches` (`:318-326`) requires the diagnostics report to equal
`expected-report-svg.json` exactly.

The raster dimension is "document parity under one rasterizer"; the official
Chromium raster is not consulted. `PINNED_RASTERIZER` (`:234-238`) and
`pins_are_measured_by_the_locked_rasterizer` (`:249-274`) fail by construction
on any resvg, tiny-skia or fontdb version change, even when no measurement
moves, and do not cover usvg's shaping stack (harfrust 0.12.0, skrifa 0.44.0,
distinct from the renderer's harfrust 0.15.0), which drives the RTL pins.

Issue #118 and ADR-0005 concern `excalidraw-render-compare`'s own closure
(`canonical-visual-v1.closure.json`), which has no normal dependencies and no
rasterizer. They do not address the shared resvg. `resvg` is a normal
dependency of the renderer (`crates/excalidraw-render/Cargo.toml:30-34`) and a
dev-dependency for tests (`:69-72`), so it sits on both sides of both
harnesses.

## 3. What the PNG harness judges

`direct_png_divergence` (`parity_registered_case_png.rs:77-150`): our PNG from
the renderer's tiny-skia pipeline, decoded straight-alpha, against
`official-png.rgba` under `ComparisonMode::Canonical`. Size mismatch is a hard
failure unless pinned in `KNOWN_EXTENT_DEFECTS` (empty).

Metrics (`comparison.rs:394-440`): a Pixelmatch 7.2.0 port with
`threshold = 0.1`, `include_aa = false`, over alpha, over-black and over-white
views, union of changed masks; SSIM (11×11 Gaussian σ=1.5) on alpha and two
luma planes, minimum of the three. Every render-tier call passes an empty crop
list. `max_delta = 352.15` means a fully covered pixel is flagged only above
about 26 grey levels.

Canonical budgets (`comparator-profiles/canonical-visual-v1.json`,
`comparison.rs:31-42`): all 48 variants are `geometry/image` → changed pixels
≤ area/400 **and** SSIM ≥ 0.999.

**No budgets were recalibrated under #195.** ADR-0014 rejects lowering the
class budgets and chooses identical-input controls for representative cases
only (spec §14.4), implemented in xtask, not in this tier. Its data: the
reference's own SVG-versus-canvas SSIM on a geometry crop was 0.919, and
Chromium versus pure-Rust 0.896. The 0.999 floor demanded of the renderer is
about 80× tighter in 1−SSIM than the reference's self-consistency.

`tolerance_exception` (`:154-165`) and `divergence()`
(`registered_cases.rs:714-732`) hold a variant to `max_changed_pixels` and
`minimum_ssim_bits` as ceilings and fail only on exceeding them or on passing
the canonical budget ("stale").

## 5. Brittleness

Twenty-six commits touched the three files between 2026-09-03 and 2026-10-30.

**Dependency bumps (3)**: `2b3bbb1f` resvg 0.45.1→0.47.0 + tiny-skia
0.11.4→0.12.0; `2d4a958d` 0.47.0→0.48.1 + fontdb 0.23→0.24, rustybuzz →
HarfRust/skrifa; `65b1f100` 0.48.1→0.49.0. In all three every pin measured
identically; the only edit was `PINNED_RASTERIZER`. `registry.json` has not
changed since 2026-09-20, so none of the 22 PNG ceilings moved either. The
empirical fragility to rasterizer churn is zero on measurements and 100% on
process.

**Renderer changes (the real churn)**, all on 2026-09-10: `cf1a0a2e` (#169
baseline snap) moved 16 ceilings at once (58 files); `3e716b04` dropped five
exceptions that then passed canonical; `dc28a6ec` moved four ceilings; the SVG
pins and PNG known defects were restructured several times within the day.
Each ceiling move edits `registry.json`, `manifest.json` and `manifest.sha256`
per case. Pins have been re-pinned about nine times for renderer changes and
zero times for dependency bumps, but the three bumps each forced a mandatory
edit.

## 6. Frame export parity

`parity_frame_export.rs`, one test, `focused.frame-export.v1` (seven targets,
profile `revision-1-frame.json`). Per frame: PNG against `official-png.rgba`
under Canonical with a class hard-coded by ordinal (`:61-65`); SVG against the
official SVG both through resvg under `PureRustSvg`; PDF checked structurally
by string search and for determinism. No exceptions or pins.

Two weaknesses: `rasterize_svg` (`:207-214`) uses `usvg::Options::default()`,
whose fontdb is empty, and usvg does not parse `@font-face` data URLs, so
frame 7's only `<text>` renders as nothing on both sides and the Typography
comparison of that frame is vacuous on the SVG side. It also returns
premultiplied data without the un-premultiply the scene harness performs.

## 7. Limits

`limits_scene_admission.rs`: a 1,048,576-entry array, a 64 MiB input and a
256 MiB parser-memory scene; inclusive-at-equality and rejected-one-over
through `common/parse_limits.rs`. Not time-based.

`limits_scene_scaling.rs`: wall-clock ceilings via `Instant::now()`
(`:125-133`): 20 s (`:34, :73`) for 49,152 long-id rectangles and 22,528
bound-text pairs, 60 s (`:106`) for a million-scalar text. They run in debug
on shared `ubuntu-latest` runners inside a shard with other tests running
concurrently. Observed 3.4–4.1 s and 22.6 s; margin 2.6–5×. No flake has been
recorded; a cold or throttled runner could trip the 20 s ceilings.

## 8. Real value and recommendations

**Protects well**: path geometry, exactly, on 48 variants; the diagnostics
contract; extent and sizing; raster on the 26 clean PNG and 43 clean SVG-raster
variants.

**Sensitivity**: text one pixel off is caught on clean variants through the
rasters, but on the 22 excepted PNG variants there is a dead zone between the
canonical budget and the ceiling (bidi-text may drift anywhere between 387 and
2375 px). A stroke color change `#1e1e1e→#333333` passes Pixelmatch on every
pixel; colors are never compared textually although the official SVG carries
`stroke="…"`. Broken rounded corners are caught exactly on every variant.

**False fires**: zero measurement moves from three rasterizer bumps, three
guaranteed `PINNED_RASTERIZER` failures; the 2026-09-10 renderer changes
required re-transcribing 20 exact ceilings across about 60 files.

Recommendations:

1. Decouple the SSIM floor from the pass gate for cross-rasterizer PNG on
   `geometry/image`, or recalibrate it from identical-input controls (the
   committed `official-svg.rgba`/`official-png.rgba` pairs). Most of the 22
   exceptions would evaporate, and the dead zone with them.
2. Make exceptions bounded two-sided tolerances rather than exact one-sided
   ceilings.
3. Add exact textual dimensions for `stroke`, `fill`, `stroke-width`,
   `opacity` and `<text x y font-size>` in document order.
4. Pin the harness rasterizer separately from the renderer's, including
   `harfrust`/`skrifa`.
5. Split carried variants into a `known-divergence` group so "parity passes"
   means the clean set.
6. Load embedded fonts in `parity_frame_export::rasterize_svg`; take the class
   from the manifest.
7. Prune `official.png` and consider compressing the 64 MB of raw rasters.
8. Make the scaling ceilings relative or run them in release.
